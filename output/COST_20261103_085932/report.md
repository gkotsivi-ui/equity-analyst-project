## 1. Overview

- Costco operates membership warehouses in the U.S., Puerto Rico, Canada, Mexico, Japan and other countries in Asia, Europe and Oceania. (10-K Item 1: "We are principally engaged in the operation of membership warehouses in the United States (U.S.) and Puerto Rico, Canada, Mexico, Japan, the United Kingdom (U.K.), Korea, Australia, Taiwan, China, Spain, France, Sweden, Iceland, and New Zealand.")
- The company earns money by selling a limited selection of branded and private-label products at low prices, which aims to produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover. (10-K Item 1: "We operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover.")
- Members pay an annual fee, which is $65 in the U.S. and includes a free household card. (10-K Item 1: "Our annual fee for memberships is $65 in the U.S., varies in other countries and includes a free household card.")

- Costco reports three operating segments, U.S., Canadian and Other International, which use generally the same operating model. (10-K Item 7: "Our operating models are generally the same across our U.S., Canadian, and Other International operating segments (see Note 11 to the consolidated financial statements included in Item 8 of this Report).")
- Its customers are paid members, who numbered 84,100 thousand at the latest year end, up from 81,000 thousand and 76,200 thousand in the two prior years. (10-K Item 1: "Paid members1 | 84,100 | 81,000 | 76,200")
- Membership fees are a separate revenue source, totaling $5,907 million in the latest fiscal year, up from $5,323 million the year before. (10-K Item 7: "Membership fees | $5,907 | $5,323 | $4,828")
- Gasoline made up 11% of total net sales in 2026. (10-K Item 1: "Our gasoline business represented 11% of total net sales in 2026.")
