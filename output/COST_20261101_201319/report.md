## 1. Overview

- Costco runs membership warehouses in the U.S. and Puerto Rico and in several other countries, including Canada, Mexico, Japan and the U.K. (10-K Item 1: "We are principally engaged in the operation of membership warehouses in the United States (U.S.) and Puerto Rico, Canada, Mexico, Japan, the United Kingdom (U.K.), Korea, Australia, Taiwan, China, Spain, France, Sweden, Iceland, and New Zealand.")

(10-K Item 1: "We operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover.")

- Costco reports three operating segments, U.S., Canadian and Other International, and they use generally the same operating model. (10-K Item 7: "Our operating models are generally the same across our U.S., Canadian, and Other International operating segments (see Note 11 to the consolidated financial statements included in Item 8 of this Report).")
- Costco had 84,100 thousand paid members at the end of 2026, up from 81,000 thousand in 2025. (10-K Item 1: "Paid members1 | 84,100 | 81,000 | 76,200")
- Executive members accounted for approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales in 2026. (10-K Item 1: "The penetration of sales to Executive members represented approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales in 2026.")

- Costco operated 939 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026, up from 914 a year earlier. (10-K Item 1: "Costco operated 939, 914, and 890 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026, August 31, 2025, and September 1, 2024.")
